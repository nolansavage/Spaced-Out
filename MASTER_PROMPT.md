# GAME OVERVIEW

SPACED OUT is a pixel-art sci-fi farming survival RPG.

The player wakes up alone aboard a massive damaged spaceship drifting through unknown space.

The ship is failing.

The crew is gone.

Humanity's future is uncertain.

The player must:

- farm
- repair
- explore
- craft
- fight
- discover
- rebuild

The spaceship becomes:

- the player's home
- the player's farm
- the player's base
- the player's companion
- humanity's final hope

The emotional journey:

"I am trapped inside a dying machine."

becomes:

"This ship is my home."

becomes:

"I rebuilt humanity's future."
